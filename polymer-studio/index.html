Engineering assumptions & reference dimensions

The generated reference image is a styling guide. This model defines a consistent five-axis chain and a new, explicit set of dimensions. The original image does not establish dimensions or prove a working mechanism.

Plastic parts include bearing housings, ribbed links, removable covers and hexagonal nut pockets. The 608 bearing envelope is 8 × 22 × 7 mm; the displayed M3 nut is 5.5 mm across flats and 2.4 mm thick. Seat and pocket allowances are provisional.

The generic servo and stepper casings show end plates, mounting ears, output horns and wired connectors. Their nominal body envelopes do not identify compatible commercial motors. Motor mounts, drive couplings, joint retention, gripper transmission and hard stops require your actual hardware. Cable loops follow their attachment points but do not simulate cable stiffness, bend-radius limits or collision avoidance. The scene does not perform collision detection, FEA or printability certification.
