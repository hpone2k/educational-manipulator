About this model Geometry & controls

A five-axis serial arm with base yaw, shoulder pitch, elbow pitch, wrist pitch and wrist roll. Gripper opening is separate from the five arm joints.

Shoulder height: 90 mm. Upper arm: 180 mm. Forearm: 150 mm. Wrist to roll axis: 52 mm. Roll axis to tool centre: 73 mm. Angles and limits are illustrative; this model is not calibrated to your motors. Shoulder zero points horizontally along +X; elbow and wrist pitch are relative to the preceding link.

Select a label or a joint on the model to highlight its control. Sliders and number fields also work with the keyboard. Camera view buttons provide alternatives to dragging. The 12-second demonstration returns to its starting pose.
