These replace the earlier metal/hybrid styling direction with printed-plastic custom structural parts: base, links, joint brackets, covers, motor mounts and gripper fingers. Motors, bearing races, shafts, screws, nuts and guide rods remain purchased hardware.

The images explore deeper link sections, internal ribs, generous joint-root fillets, double-supported pivots, reinforced fastener bosses and cable routing. They are generated visual studies, not CAD assemblies or verified engineering diagrams. Some illustrative joint labels, fasteners and inset details may be imprecise. Five-axis kinematics, detailed interfaces, print orientation, tolerances, layer-direction strength, creep, deflection, motor torque and base anchoring must be established in the actual design. No load rating is implied.
